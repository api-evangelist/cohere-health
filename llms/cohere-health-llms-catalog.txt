# Cohere Health

> Cohere Health is a United States clinical-intelligence and utilization-management company that digitizes prior authorization for health plans. Its Unify platform applies clinical AI and evidence-based policy to streamline authorization intake, review, and decisioning. Its API suite implements the HL7 Da Vinci Burden Reduction guides — Coverage Requirements Discovery (CRD), Documentation Templates and Rules (DTR), and Prior Authorization Support (PAS) — to help payers meet the CMS Interoperability and Prior Authorization Final Rule (CMS-0057-F). The FHIR R4 APIs support SMART on FHIR and are sold to / embedded with health plans rather than offered through a self-serve public developer portal; the documented product surface is real but the API endpoints are partner/health-plan gated.

## APIs
- [Coverage Requirements Discovery (CRD) API](https://www.coherehealth.com/utilization-management/api-based): Instantly verify whether prior authorization is required and confirm service coverage at the point of care (HL7 Da Vinci CRD, FHIR R4, SMART on FHIR). Partner/health-plan gated.
- [Documentation Templates and Rules (DTR) API](https://www.coherehealth.com/utilization-management/api-based): Intelligently gather and submit the documentation a prior authorization request requires, driven by digitized medical policy (FHIR Questionnaire + CQL; HL7 Da Vinci DTR). Partner/health-plan gated.
- [Prior Authorization Support (PAS) API](https://www.coherehealth.com/utilization-management/api-based): Submit an authorization request and return the outcome, wrapping X12 278 in a FHIR interface for CMS-0057-F compliance (HL7 Da Vinci PAS). Partner/health-plan gated.

## Standards & Conformance
- HL7 Da Vinci Burden Reduction Implementation Guides: CRD, DTR, PAS (FHIR R4)
- SMART on FHIR (OAuth2) application support
- X12 278 utilization-management exchange (wrapped by PAS)
- CMS-0057-F (Interoperability and Prior Authorization Final Rule) alignment
- OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect on the platform login host (PKCE S256)

## Identity & Auth
- [OpenID Connect discovery](https://login.coherehealth.com/.well-known/openid-configuration): Okta-backed platform login (login.coherehealth.com). SMART-on-FHIR API endpoints/scopes are provisioned per health-plan tenant and not published anonymously.

## Security & Compliance
- [HITRUST r2 Certification](https://www.coherehealth.com/news/cohere-health-earns-hitrust-r2-certification): Risk-based 2-year certification of the UM platform (first earned 2022-08-17); covers ISO, NIST, PCI, HIPAA, GDPR standards.
